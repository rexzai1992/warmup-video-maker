# Handoff: where we stopped (14 Nov 2026)

Read this first when continuing on another PC.

## Done
- **Two 15-minute versions:** `school` (14.5 min) and `japan` (13.4 min). Each part has its move, footage, on-screen text and voice lines (`timelines/*.js`).
- **Character:** both versions now use the **line character**, a simple faceless figure with a dark body and a glowing white outline, like the reference YouTube video. The older characters still work: set `"character": "kid"` or `"hana"` in the timeline.
- **Smoother look:** the body blends between moves, scenes crossfade instead of flashing white, the camera drifts slowly over the footage, and the video runs at 30 fps.
- **Sound:** a beat-synced soundtrack at 120 BPM (`engine/synth.py`): music, sound effects, a countdown, and the music cutting out during freezes. Both soundtracks are already built in `audio/`.
- **Google Flow scenes are supported:** put a clip at `footage/flow/<version>/<part id>.mp4` (example: `footage/flow/school/stretch.mp4`). It automatically replaces the stock footage for that part.
- **Player:** `player/Start_Warmup.bat` plays the video full screen in Chrome, on repeat.
