# YouTube uploads: titles, descriptions, tags

Files: `output/youtube/school_ch1.mp4` … `school_ch5.mp4`, `japan_ch1.mp4` … `japan_ch5.mp4`
(make them with `python make_video.py school --chapters` and `python make_video.py japan --chapters`).

## Settings for EVERY upload
- **Audience: "Yes, it's made for kids"** (required by law, COPPA).
- **Altered or synthetic content: "Yes".** The backgrounds are realistic AI scenes (Google Flow). YouTube asks you to tick this for realistic AI scenes.
- Category: **Education** (or Sports). Language: English.
- Add every chapter video to one playlist per theme: "Back to School Warm-Up" and "Japan Adventure Warm-Up".
- Upload 2–3 per week rather than all at once. Change the thumbnail text for each one.
